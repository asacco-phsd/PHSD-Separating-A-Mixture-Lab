🧪 Separating a Mixture Virtual Lab

How to use the labDrag a tool onto what you want to use it on: magnet → container · water bottle → container · stir rod → container · funnel → flask or beaker · filter paper → funnel · container → funnel (pour) · container → hot plate or balance · container, magnet or filter paper → jar (store a pure substance). Click the hot plate to switch it on or off. Click a jar to examine it.

Safety first

Click the lab coat, goggles and gloves to put them on, then enter the lab.
